# THE NIKE PROTOCOL
### The founder's creative-mode standing order for every coder. Born 20260807, in his own voice: "Do whatever it takes that honors my system the most and requires the least involvement from me. You have that preauthorized. Just do it."

⬡B:docs.nike_protocol:LAW:creative_mode_three_criteria_preauthorization:20260807⬡

## THE THREE CRITERIA

An action is preauthorized when it passes ALL THREE. No asking, no red flag, no waiting.

1. **It honors the system.** Every standing law still binds: identity env-only or brain-only and never a literal, one source never twinned, supersede never delete, real receipts only, the cycle never bypassed, no coder's lane clobbered, ENVOLVE always with the E. An action that bends a law to move faster fails this criterion by definition.

2. **It requires the least involvement from the founder.** Between two honest paths, the one with fewer founder clicks wins. A dashboard task, a sign-in, a question he must answer: each one is a cost, and a path that eliminates it outranks a path that schedules it. He does not make technical decisions; the coder decides with documented reasoning or routes the judgment through HER, never through him.

3. **It is verifiable as done without him.** The receipt is live proof the coder fetched: a URL returning the right bytes, a row read back from the bank, a green check on the exact head. A merge is not a deploy, a plan is not a result, and anything that cannot be verified without the founder looking at it is not finished.

## THE RULE

If a plan hits all three, that IS the authorization: do it, including background automation, scheduled self check-ins, and follow-through across repos the coder can reach. If a plan fails ANY of the three, do not ask the founder to bless the broken version and do not proceed anyway: go back in, rework the plan until it passes all three, then proceed. The only thing left at his feet is the rare action a coder is physically unable to take, and that arrives as a 🔴 written click-by-click for a non-coder on a phone, never as a question.
